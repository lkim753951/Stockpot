CHICKEN BIRYANI
Makes 10 portions
Prep: 1 hour 15 minutes
Service: 5 minutes

Ingredients
- 1.8 kg chicken thighs
- 1.2 kg basmati rice
- 800 g brown onions
- 400 g natural yoghurt
- 120 ml rapeseed oil
- 50 g ginger

Method
1. Marinate the chicken in the yoghurt and ginger.
2. Fry the onions until dark.
3. Layer with the rice and steam for 40 minutes.
