Stockpot privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Stockpot helps independent restaurants and cafés check prices, deliveries, invoices and daily checks. It was built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026.

What Stockpot stores

  • Restaurant records: suppliers, recipes, prices, orders, deliveries, invoices, sales, checklists and tasks.
  • Staff details: the name, language and WhatsApp number the restaurant adds.
  • Messages, voice notes and photos that staff send to the restaurant's Stockpot number.
  • Supplier call recordings and transcripts, when the owner records or uploads a call.
  • Photos, videos and documents added to the app.
  • Supplier emails with invoices, if the owner connects Gmail.

Why it's used

Stockpot uses this information to run the restaurant's checks, send reminders and alerts, and show the owner what needs attention. It isn't sold, and it isn't used for advertising.

Who handles it

  • Meta runs the AI models and delivers WhatsApp messages.
  • Supabase stores data and files in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Vercel hosts the site in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Google provides Gmail access, only if the owner connects it.
  • Twilio places alert calls, only if a restaurant sets them up.

Calls

The owner must confirm that the other person has been told about the recording before Stockpot starts listening.

How long it's kept

Records stay until the restaurant removes them. Stockpot also keeps its own backups in private storage.

Your choices

Staff can ask their restaurant to correct or remove their details and messages. Anyone can also ask for their data to be removed by opening an issue on the Stockpot GitHub page. Don't include personal details in the issue, because issues are public.

Questions

Ask through the Stockpot GitHub page.
