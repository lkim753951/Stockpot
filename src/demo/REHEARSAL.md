# Rehearsal: the story of one order

Stageshow is for demos, not real app screens, so only `Stories` and `FeatureGrid` use its kit. The app screens borrow its written rules and nothing else.

Six scenes follow one chicken thigh order, so the money saved carries from scene to scene. The scenes share one stage size, so nothing jumps.

Gold: `bento` and `tilted-stack` for the feature grid.
Beats: the shoebox, the call, the filmed count, the Polish voice note reaching the owner, the invoice, the total. Each scene plays once, about 8 seconds.
Captured: 1280 and 390 wide. Light theme filmed. Dark checked by screenshot only.

Film:
- `kit override` (6 lines) on every run: false alarm. The kit's own CSS is bundled with the app's, so film can't tell it is the kit's. The kit files are unchanged.
- `inconclusive`: sampling gaps in this headless environment, so motion between frames wasn't fully judged.
- `touching` on the receipt pile: the slips overlap on purpose. Looked at the frames and kept it.

Review: a fresh-eye pass on the earlier window-and-phone build asked for a phone instead of a window, a real list with no void, no clock on the owner's phone, a fuller receipt, WhatsApp colours, one fridge story and a sign that it's a call. All done. The receipt tile stays a still. The rebuilt hero wasn't reviewed again.

Not checked: a real phone, Safari, and the loop on a slow device.
